About Amber Fort

Amber Fort (also written Amer) sits on a ridge about 11 km north of Jaipur, its walls reflected in Maota Lake below. Courtyards, gateways and palaces climb the hillside, mixing red sandstone with white marble.

The Sheesh Mahal, or mirror palace, is the highlight: its walls and ceilings are set with tiny mirrors and coloured glass that multiply the light of a single lamp. Ganesh Pol, a painted gateway, leads into the private apartments.

History

  1. 12th centuryThe Kachhwaha Rajputs establish their seat at Amer.
  2. 1592Man Singh I begins the palace that stands today.
  3. 1600sJai Singh I adds halls, gardens and the Sheesh Mahal.
  4. 1727Sawai Jai Singh II moves the capital to the new city of Jaipur.
  5. 2013Amber is inscribed with five other Rajasthan forts as the Hill Forts of Rajasthan.

What to look for

  • Sheesh Mahal and its mirrored ceiling
  • Ganesh Pol with its painted arch
  • Sila Devi temple near Suraj Pol
  • Kesar Kyari, the saffron garden set into Maota Lake
