About City Palace

City Palace stands at the heart of the old city, a sequence of courtyards, pavilions and gardens. Part of it is still the residence of the Jaipur royal family, while the rest is a museum of textiles, weapons, paintings and royal costume.

Pritam Niwas Chowk has four painted doorways, one for each season, with the Peacock Gate the best known. In the Diwan-i-Khas stand two enormous silver urns, made for Madho Singh II so he could carry Ganges water with him on a 1902 voyage to England.

History

  1. 1727Jai Singh II lays out Jaipur and chooses a site for his palace.
  2. 1729 to 1732The main palace complex is built.
  3. 1900Mubarak Mahal, a reception hall, is completed under Madho Singh II.
  4. 1902The giant silver urns are made for the maharaja's England journey.
  5. 1949Jaipur joins the state of Rajasthan; the family keeps Chandra Mahal as a residence.

What to look for

  • The two silver urns in the Diwan-i-Khas
  • Peacock Gate in Pritam Niwas Chowk
  • Chandra Mahal, the seven-storey residence
  • The textile and arms galleries
