About Govind Dev Ji Temple

This Krishna temple is set inside the City Palace grounds, in the garden between Chandra Mahal and the lake. Its image is one of the most revered in Jaipur, and thousands of devotees come daily.

The deity is dressed and decorated differently through the day, and darshan is offered in short openings. Janmashtami, Krishna's birthday, is the biggest festival, when the whole temple is decorated with flowers and lights.

History

  1. Late 1600sThe image is moved from Vrindavan for safekeeping.
  2. Early 1700sJai Singh II installs it in Jaipur, close to his palace.
  3. TodayIt remains one of the most visited temples in the city.

What to look for

  • The morning aarti
  • Krishna's changing costumes and decorations
  • Janmashtami celebrations
  • The large pillared hall
