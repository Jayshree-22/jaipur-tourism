About Albert Hall Museum

Albert Hall is a museum in Ram Niwas Garden, designed in the Indo-Saracenic style that blends Rajput and Mughal forms with Victorian planning. Arcades, domes and chhatris frame a central arched hall.

Inside are miniature paintings, carpets, metalwork, ivory carvings, sculptures and an Egyptian mummy. The building is floodlit each evening and makes a striking picture against the night sky.

History

  1. 1876The Prince of Wales lays the foundation stone during his visit to Jaipur.
  2. 1887The building opens as a public museum under Madho Singh II.
  3. 1900sCollections of arts and crafts are added.
  4. TodayIt is one of the state's oldest museums.

What to look for

  • The central arched hall and courtyard
  • Miniature paintings and carpets
  • The Egyptian mummy
  • The building lit up at night
