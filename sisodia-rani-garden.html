About Sisodia Rani Garden

Sisodia Rani Garden was laid out on the slopes east of Jaipur as a retreat for a queen from Udaipur. Terraces step down the hillside with fountains, water channels and tall trees.

Pavilions on the upper terraces hold murals of Radha and Krishna and scenes of hunting and courtly life. The garden is especially green in the monsoon, when the fountains run at full strength.

History

  1. 1728Jai Singh II builds the garden and palace for his Sisodia queen.
  2. 1700sThe murals and pavilions are decorated.
  3. TodayThe garden is a peaceful escape from the busy city.

What to look for

  • The terraced fountains
  • Radha-Krishna murals in the pavilions
  • Shady walks in the upper garden
  • Monsoon greenery
