About Isarlat (Sargasuli)

Isarlat stands near Tripolia Bazaar, a tall tower that once dominated the Jaipur skyline. Its Hindi nickname, Sargasuli, means "heaven-piercing".

A narrow stair leads to the top, where you can look across the old city, the palace and the hills. The tower was built to mark a military victory and became a landmark of the walled city.

History

  1. 1743Jai Singh II dies; his sons Ishwari Singh and Madho Singh contest the throne.
  2. 1747Ishwari Singh prevails in battle near Rajamahal.
  3. 1749He builds Isarlat to commemorate the victory.
  4. 1750Ishwari Singh dies; accounts say by his own hand.

What to look for

  • The stair to the top
  • Views of City Palace and Hawa Mahal
  • Old-city rooftops at sunset
  • Tripolia Bazaar below
