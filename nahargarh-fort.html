About Nahargarh Fort

Nahargarh guards the northern edge of Jaipur from the Aravalli ridge. It was built as a defensive outpost and hunting retreat, and its walls run along the crest of the hill.

Inside, Madhavendra Bhawan is a summer palace with identical suites for the maharaja's queens, joined by a shared corridor. From the ramparts you can see the whole of the old city, Jal Mahal and the plains beyond.

History

  1. 1734Jai Singh II builds the fort to strengthen Jaipur's defences.
  2. 1880sMadho Singh II adds Madhavendra Bhawan.
  3. 1900sThe fort becomes a royal retreat and picnic destination.
  4. TodayIt is one of the city's most popular sunset viewpoints.

What to look for

  • Madhavendra Bhawan and its courtyard
  • The step-well near the entrance
  • Ramparts with views over Jaipur
  • The legend of the spirit Nahar Singh Bhomia
